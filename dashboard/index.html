    DECLARED REQUIREMENTS

    What earns a valid result?

    • Three blocks of 24 matched pairs per control.
    • Block ratios and intervals within the declared 3% bound.
    • Starting-order bias within tolerance in every block.
    • All six inputs pass CPU and wall, then independent confirmation.

    Failed controls remain in the evidence. Thresholds are not relaxed after seeing results.

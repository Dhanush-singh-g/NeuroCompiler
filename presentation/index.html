What do these optimizations do?
O3
LLVM's standard high-optimization pipeline.
GVN
Reuses equivalent calculations instead of computing them again.
DCE
Removes calculations whose results are unused.
LICM
Moves a repeated calculation outside a loop when that is safe.
mem2reg / loop-rotate
Converts eligible local memory operations into SSA values and reshapes loops to help later optimization.
instcombine
Simplifies and combines instructions using valid algebraic rules.

O3 may already perform the useful work, so different pipelines can produce identical executables.

What remains a research objective

Reliable improvement over O3 on genuinely unseen programs. The experimental model failed deployment gates; the approved policy remains O3. Live inputs are not untouched research tests or new training labels. Instruction-count SL/RL/hybrid modes are experimental. Runtime-trained RL and reliable real-program generalization remain future work.

What this demonstration can establish

A working path from your source to features, a learned prediction, executable pipelines, input-specific output checks, and an exploratory comparison. An inconclusive result is kept visible.

What remains a research objective

Reliable improvement over O3 on genuinely unseen programs. The experimental model failed deployment gates; the approved policy remains O3. Live inputs are not untouched research tests or new training labels. RL and hybrid learning remain future work.
